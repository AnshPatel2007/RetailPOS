How this was checked

An automated browser signed in as each role on an isolated test database seeded with a realistic store (34 products, a carton pack, a variety pack, customers, a morning of sales, a refund, a void, expenses and a promotion). It opened every page that role can reach, took screenshots, measured overflow and control sizes, and ran an accessibility scan (WCAG 2 A/AA). Tasks were driven the way a cashier or owner would, counting each click, key, typed entry and scan. {{UNLABELED}} form fields without labels were also found.

Manager screens were checked too (56 pages). They match the admin screens shown here, except Staff, Payroll, Reconciliation and Background Jobs, which managers cannot open, so they are not repeated in the gallery.

Screens marked "low-contrast" fail the contrast check in at least one place; most share the same few elements, so one color change fixes most of them.
