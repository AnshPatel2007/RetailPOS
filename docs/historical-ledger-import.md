# Historical ledger summaries

Historical ledgers can contain daily collections without receipt lines or merchandise costs. These are stored as `Sale.sourceType = DAILY_SUMMARY`; normal checkouts default to `RECEIPT`. `sourceData` preserves the business date, original register figures, daily tax components, card amount, lottery net, source hash/line and caveats. A stored midnight represents the business date in the store timezone, not a checkout time.

Reports include recorded sales and daily taxes once. Daily summaries do not create item lines, inventory movements, drawer entries, customer spending or loyalty activity. They are excluded from staff/hourly attribution and cannot be refunded or voided as receipts. Receipt counts, average orders, discounts and merchandise costs remain unavailable when summaries are included. Card allocations are recorded separately; the remaining tender is unspecified, never inferred as cash. CSV exports label summaries, omit fictional checkout timestamps and mark missing discounts unavailable.

Monthly source expenses use `Expense.sourceType = MONTHLY_SUMMARY`, are recognized at month end, and have no asserted payment date. The approved status recognizes the expense; it does not indicate a payment. Monthly gaming/ATM and lottery commission income is held in `HistoricalIncome`, separately from sales. Lottery net means sales minus payouts and is not commission income. Missing lottery net cells remain unknown.

`backend/scripts/import-historical-ledger.cjs` exports `buildPlan` and `applyPlan`. The plan accepts verified store/user identities and integer-cent source facts. Its deterministic keys and transaction lock prevent duplicate retries. Existing records must match the original facts; retries never overwrite edits. Any conflict rolls back the entire transaction. The module does not access payment providers or send notifications.

The October 22, 2026 local import source, preflight review, database backup reference and reconciliation are retained under `.local/imports/store-ledgers-411140c2dc54/`. The operational runner is `.local/import-store-ledgers.cjs` (dry run by default; `--apply` writes). It restricts itself to the authorized local database, checks the backup checksum and store identities, and verifies that setup, stock, customers, shifts and drawer entries remain unchanged.

Imported coverage: Nelson Food & Liquor, January–June 2026 (181 days); A&P Food and Liquor, January–September 2026 (273 days). The 17 expense entries include Nelson January's three separately listed additional costs totaling $11,000. There are 28 monthly income entries. Supplied gross/net profits, assumed merchandise margins, unclassified “Tax Profit”, an unexplained $500 January tax adjustment, unclassified bank movements and one out-of-period zero placeholder were excluded. Merchandise costs are unavailable, so gross and net profit remain unavailable.

P&L uses recorded inventory and sale costs only. The fixed percentage estimate has been removed. Historical costs and dependent profit totals remain unavailable. A month selector scopes sales, expenses and other income together; clearing it returns to year to date. See inventory-costing.md for the opening review, receiving and correction workflows.

Export consistency: direct CSV/PDF, previews, accounting journals and queued sales exports identify daily summaries. Queued exports omit fictional checkout timestamps and importing-operator cashier attribution. Summary tender labels distinguish the known card component from the unspecified remainder. Accounting journals identify unknown settlement rather than assigning those summaries to a cash account. Queued monthly expense exports include source type and the month-end recognition convention. Export previews use the report calendar's date rather than the browser's timezone. Export generation does not write estimated merchandise costs into accounting facts.

P&L Excel: Financial Management's **Export Excel** action creates a native `.xlsx` workbook with one sheet per store/month, following the supplied ledger layout: daily sales/register/tax/card/lottery columns on the left, monthly sales, expenses, other income, profit and margins on the right. Multi-month exports include full-period totals. The current month/store selection is preserved; export reads a fresh repeatable-read snapshot and updates the displayed statement to that same snapshot. The workbook identifies the store, timezone, inclusive dates, snapshot and selected cost basis. Unknown register allocations stay blank, missing costs/profit remain unavailable, and monthly expenses never receive invented daily dates. Payroll is aggregated without employee descriptions. Returns/voids follow their actual reversal periods and appear in a dated adjustment table. Amounts are numeric, losses use red parentheses, totals/profit use formulas with cached results, headings freeze during scrolling, and ordinary months fit one printed landscape page. Source strings cannot become formulas. Export is disabled during loading/errors, cannot overlap, and is cancelled on navigation, scope or session changes. Workbook detail requests are bounded to a year and 120 store-months; only existing P&L-authorized roles can request them. ExcelJS is loaded only when exporting.

Verification: historicalSummary.workflow.test.ts covers reconciliation, isolation, retry/rollback behavior, unchanged stock/drawers and recorded-only compatibility. Historical-summary and P&L browser tests cover source labels, unknown metrics, old links, month selection and Excel downloads. Inventory costing verification is documented in inventory-costing.md.
