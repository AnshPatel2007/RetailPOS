# Inventory costing and recorded P&L

Financial Management → Inventory costs contains opening review, valuation as of a store date, missing costs, the value ledger and monthly reconciliation. Owners confirm physical quantities and documented values at cost. Suggested opening values use the latest supplier price only as a starting point for review. Confirming an opening does not assign costs to earlier sales. Zero is a confirmed free cost; an unknown value remains unavailable.

Opening setup is a starting point for existing stock, not a monthly reset. Monthly closing quantities and values carry forward. Newly received stock with recorded costs needs no additional opening approval. The value ledger remains available to trace inventory value and COGS, investigate discrepancies, and preview audited corrections.

Owners can choose **Review opening inventory** to review up to 500 pending products together, including results beyond the balances table's first page. Product search limits the group. Empty inventory, stock with recorded values, and previously confirmed openings are excluded. The review shows 25 editable rows per page; selection and edits persist between pages. Owners can exclude products lacking evidence, edit quantities and total values, enter one supporting-document reference, attest to the selected group, and confirm once. Zero supplier prices leave the suggested value blank until a documented zero or other value is supplied. Changing quantity does not automatically change the reviewed total value. For larger inventories, confirm one group and reopen for the next. Cancel makes no changes. Confirmation uses one atomic, owner-only operation with stock-version checks and a durable retry key; a stale line rejects the entire group, while lost-response retries cannot duplicate openings.

Receive merchandise through purchase orders or reviewed invoice imports. Both post through the same receiving service, capture pack conversions and actual costs, and allocate discounts, freight and nonrecoverable acquisition taxes by merchandise value with deterministic rounding. Only accepted units enter stock. Invoice dates are stored separately from receipt dates. Supplier invoice numbers and request keys prevent duplicate posting. Inventory-purchase expense entries can link to their receipt and are excluded from operating expenses.

Tracked merchandise uses moving weighted-average cost per product/store. Ten units at $8 plus ten at $10 carry $180 at a $9 average. Selling three at $15 records $27 COGS, $18 gross profit and $153 remaining inventory. Changing the catalog purchase price does not change carrying value or booked sale costs. Decimal calculations retain eight places and the final depletion consumes the rounding remainder.

Returns of sellable goods restore the original recorded sale cost. Money-only refunds do not restore stock; the sale remains available for a later physical item return. Bulk merchandise returns explicitly return all outstanding units. Unsellable goods retain their original sale expense and have a separate loss record without a second cost deduction. Voids restore inventory at the original cost. Transfers preserve dispatch cost through receipt, return and in-transit balances; documented damage is a separate loss.

Counts use the recorded average for shortages and surpluses. Unexplained additions stay incomplete until an owner documents their value. Unknown opening inventory cannot become known just because a later priced delivery arrives. Checkout remains available with missing costs. Offline costs remain unverified until original evidence is reviewed; any difference from physical depletion appears as an inventory-cost adjustment. Nontracked merchandise has separate sale-cost records, which owners can resolve without creating stock.

Use **Review cost** on the original opening, receipt or documented addition to preview a correction. This also handles supplier price credits without a physical return. The preview includes affected inventory and sale costs, including transfers. Confirmation appends immutable correction/revision records and restates the affected periods; booked sale lines are not edited. A changed inventory snapshot requires a fresh preview. Interactive corrections are bounded to 25,000 later movements; larger period corrections require a separately reviewed migration. Physical supplier returns remove current carrying value and show the difference from the supplier credit as a purchase-cost adjustment.

P&L, dashboards, analysis, assistant tools and Excel use shared financial metrics: net sales excluding tax; recorded sale costs net of returned goods; inventory-cost adjustments; total COGS; gross profit and margin; operating expenses; other income; and net income. Missing required costs make dependent profit totals unavailable while known sales and expenses remain visible. Inventory received is a reference amount, not another operating expense. Reconciliation links each movement group to its period-filtered ledger records and shows opening, movement, closing and in-transit values.

Excel retains the daily sales/register/tax/card/lottery grid and monthly side summary. Costs and reconciliation use recorded facts. The compatibility response retains `estimate: null` and `defaultCostView: "recorded"`; old `pnlCosts` links preserve their report/month without enabling estimates.

Owners approve openings and corrections. Managers receive goods, count inventory, record supplier returns and read permitted reports. Cashiers sell without cost-edit access. Platform inspection is read-only. Saved cost operations preserve their exact request across reloads for retry after a lost response.
